How signals are generated

Indicators on closed candles: EMA 20/50/200, RSI 14, ATR 14, 20-bar high/low, swing pivots.
Breakout — candle closes beyond the prior 20-bar high/low, not against the trend.
Trend Pullback — EMA50 above/below EMA200, price dips to EMA20 and closes back through it.
Range Reversal — RSI leaves an extreme (<30 / >70) with a confirming candle.
Stop = beyond the latest swing ± 0.25 ATR (kept between 1 and 3 ATR). Target = 2R (1.5R for reversals).
The confluence score counts how many conditions agree. It is not a win probability.

Analyst — weighs ten factors across two timeframes: higher-timeframe trend (±3) and MACD (±1), EMA stack (±2), swing structure (±2), MACD histogram (±1.5), RSI (±1), price vs EMA20 (±1), candle pattern (±1), volume (±0.5), nearby support/resistance (±1.5). Total ≥ +3.5 is BUY, ≤ −3.5 is SELL (4.5 when ADX is under 18), otherwise WAIT with breakout triggers. Stop = beyond the last swing (1–3 ATR), TP1 = 1.5R, TP2 = 2.5R. Confidence scales with the score; it is not a win probability either.

OpenDesk is free, open-source educational software (MIT). Signals are mechanical rule outputs, not financial advice, and can be wrong. News coverage is the scheduled economic calendar only — it does not read headlines. Data: Kraken (forex), Binance (crypto, gold), Forex Factory calendar.
